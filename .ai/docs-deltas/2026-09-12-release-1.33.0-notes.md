## Root cause of the blocked receipt (pre-existing, not introduced by this release)

`docs/architecture/architecture.html`'s embedded stylesheet is stamped `archify 2.12.0` — the
version last used to deliver it. The freshly-installed **2.17** adds (or has tightened)
`composition/desktop-readability`: a *global* check that the diagram's `meta.viewBox` width,
scaled to a 1440px desktop viewport, keeps every node's rendered text at or above 6px. `architecture.json`'s
`viewBox` is `[2260, 980]`; at that width the scale factor is `930/2260 ≈ 0.41`, so **even the
sublabel font's own preferred size (9px) projects to 3.7px** — under the 6px floor regardless of
what any node's text says. This is a structural property of the diagram's overall width, not
something a per-node width or sublabel edit can fix.

**Proof this predates 1.33.0, not my edit:** reproduced on the *unmodified* base
(`git show HEAD:docs/architecture/architecture.json`, before this session's edits) with only the
minimum patch needed to reach the composition-check stage (shortening the one sublabel that was
already too long for its box pre-1.33.0, and shortening the boundary label) — same
`composition/desktop-readability` failure, same evidence shape. Receipt:
`2026-09-12-release-1.33.0-compare-attempt.json` (the real `compare` invocation, base = true
pre-edit `HEAD`, head = this session's edited file) fails at the `input` stage for the identical
reason before it can even reach a diff. `dataflow.json` shows the sibling failure mode: 6 of 7
render-stage sublabel-width errors are reproducible on pristine `HEAD` (`ai-specs`,
`npm-test-validators`, `implementation-branch`, `evals-scenarios`, `changelog-stamps`,
`machine-sessions`), plus one pre-existing `clean-flow/endpoint-side-direction` routing error. Only
the 7th (`memory-files`, my edit) was new, and it is now fixed (`width: 165` added).
`workflow.json` and `sequence.json` fail `validate --quality showcase` too, entirely on pre-existing
content I did not touch this session. Only `lifecycle.json` currently passes clean.

**Why this was not repaired in this pass:** the fix is a full re-layout of a 12-component,
`[2260,980]`-viewBox diagram (`architecture`) plus at least three more (`dataflow`, `workflow`,
`sequence`) — reducing the authored `viewBox` to fit a 1440px desktop reader, or splitting each into
narrower views, which means repositioning most nodes and re-routing most connections. That is a
distinct, unbounded task from a content-only release delta and risks introducing new errors without
dedicated layout time; the authoring loop's own rule — "if two consecutive rounds do not improve the
error count, stop and report" — was honored: round 1 (fixing my own added sublabel/tag widths)
eliminated every `layout/constraint` error I introduced; round 2 (attempting compare with a
minimally-patched base) confirmed the remaining failure is structural and outside a bounded repair.
Reported to the lead rather than silently declared done, and rather than quietly widened into an
undocumented re-layout of four diagrams.
